# Reference notes (from the user's sample material)

## Sierra Chest screenshots (140 screens, native 320x200)
All Act 1 to 5 rooms; used to build the backgrounds. Not stored in the repository.

## Video "The hidden pathfinding in Space Quest III" (152 s, portrait, narrated)
- Roger at full size (the zoomed sections): short orange-brown hair, pink face; grey jumpsuit, magenta left sleeve and a magenta stripe
  down the front; **black belt with a small grey pouch on the hip**; grey trousers; **tall black boots**; pink hands.
- Movement: Roger walks in a **straight line to the click and stops dead at the first obstacle**. There is no routing around things. (Our game: straight walk, clamped to the walk area. Keep.)
- Poses seen: walking, turning (3/4 back), dropping to hands and knees, crawling, **lying flat and poking with the Orat-on-a-Stick** (about 3 steps, 1.5 s). No true front or back view, no climbing, no picking up.
- Act 2 animations: the Arnoid droid (cyan figure) is caught in the gears (sparks, falls apart, pieces drop) or is lifted by a pod; a pod is a red, spider-like creature that lowers a red tentacle, wraps the droid and pulls it up.
- Interface of the original, not copied: white "Enter input" box, white speech boxes.
- **Sound:** the audio is almost entirely a narrator; the game's own sounds are drowned out. A few short tones (around 1.4 kHz, and 0.8 to 2.6 kHz blips) were found, with no reliable link to events. Sound will be designed from scratch (synthesised): pickup blip, door, danger buzz, takeoff sweep, death jingle, plus a few chiptune loops.
